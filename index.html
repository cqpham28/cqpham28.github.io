Research Interests

I have a strong interest in data-driven healthcare research integrating signal processing and machine learning, with a focus on developing efficient AI for biomedical applications.

Current Research Focus: Digital phenotyping, AI for mental health, multimodal biosignal analysis, biomedical signal processing, and development of Software as a Medical Device (SaMD).
